Conventions¶

  • Radial coordinate: normalized toroidal flux s, fixed boundary at s = 1.

  • Fourier phases: 2*pi*(m*theta - n*zeta/N_T) for spectra.

  • Boozer exports are left-handed with the pyGVEC CAS3D frame (winding -1); Equilibrium.coordinate_handedness reports the reconstructed chart.

  • Eigenvalues of StabilityProblem are omega^2 in s^-2 with mass normalization x.T @ M @ x = 1; negative values are unstable.

  • Mercier: positive D_Mercier is stable.

  • Regular displacements behave like xi^s ~ s^(|m|/2) at the axis; the FEEC trial space carries this factor for the normal and tangential components.
