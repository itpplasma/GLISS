TERPSICHORE compatibility¶

solve_terpsichore_fixed_boundary and solve_terpsichore_pseudoplasma replay the matrices stored in TERPSICHORE FORT.23 (and FORT.24) files and certify the lowest eigenpair with GLISS’s inertia bracketing. They reproduce TERPSICHORE’s eigenvalue to 1e-8 relative on the QAS3 family (benchmarks/qas3). A stable file returns negative_count == 0 with the lowest nonnegative eigenpair.

These replays validate GLISS against TERPSICHORE’s own discretization and normalization. They are not GLISS’s physical fixed-boundary operator and not a production plasma-vacuum solve.
